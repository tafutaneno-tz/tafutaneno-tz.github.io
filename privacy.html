Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Tafuta Neno is a Kiswahili word search game. This policy explains what information the game uses. In short: we do not collect any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no registration. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts or photos, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of data only on your own device:

  • which levels you have unlocked,
  • the bonus words you have found,
  • the days you completed the daily puzzle (for your streak),
  • whether sound is turned on or off.

The game also keeps a copy of its own files (pictures, sounds) on your device so it opens faster and uses less mobile data. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Third parties and advertising

The current version of the game contains no advertising and shares no data with third parties. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and it will describe which advertising provider is used and what data it may process.

Children

The game is suitable for players of all ages. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Sera ya Faragha

Imesasishwa: 7 Oktoba 2026

Tafuta Neno ni mchezo wa kutafuta maneno kwa Kiswahili. Sera hii inaeleza taarifa ambazo mchezo unatumia. Kwa ufupi: hatukusanyi taarifa zozote binafsi.

Taarifa tunazokusanya

Hakuna. Mchezo hauna akaunti wala usajili. Hatuombi jina lako, barua pepe, namba ya simu, mahali ulipo, anwani au picha zako, na hatutumii zana za kufuatilia matumizi.

Taarifa zinazohifadhiwa kwenye simu yako

Ili kukumbuka maendeleo yako, mchezo unahifadhi taarifa chache kwenye simu yako pekee:

  • viwango ulivyofungua,
  • maneno ya ziada uliyopata,
  • siku ulizomaliza fumbo la kila siku,
  • kama sauti imewashwa au imezimwa.

Mchezo pia unahifadhi nakala ya faili zake (picha, sauti) kwenye simu ili ufunguke haraka na utumie data kidogo. Hakuna kati ya haya kinachotumwa kwetu wala kwa mtu mwingine. Unaweza kufuta taarifa hizi wakati wowote kwa kufuta hifadhi ya programu au kuiondoa.

Watu wengine na matangazo

Toleo hili la mchezo halina matangazo na halishiriki taarifa na watu wengine. Matangazo yakiongezwa baadaye, sera hii itasasishwa kabla ya toleo hilo kutolewa.

Watoto

Mchezo unafaa kwa wachezaji wa umri wote. Kwa kuwa hatukusanyi taarifa binafsi za mtu yeyote, hatukusanyi taarifa za watoto.

Mabadiliko

Sera hii ikibadilika, toleo jipya litawekwa kwenye ukurasa huu pamoja na tarehe mpya.
